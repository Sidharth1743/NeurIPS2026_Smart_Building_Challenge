# PRIORS — every place we decide for the agent

Register required by `AGENTS.md`, "The reward is the only teacher" (2026-10-25).
A **prior** is any human decision in the pipeline that tells the agent what to do instead of letting the
environment's reward decide. This file lists every one I can find, what it decides, the symptom it actually
produced, its standard alternative with real values, and whether a demonstrated solution exists.

Read with: `Experiments.md` (what happened), `complete_port/docs/HISTORY.md` (when), `REFERENCE.md` (baselines).

## 0. How to read a row

- **standard alternative** — what an undecorated PPO on this contract would do, with the real value.
- **solution** — `yes` only when the alternative is both standard and demonstrated on our data; `none` means I do
  not have one that meets both tests. `no evidence` means the standard alternative exists but our own record says
  it scored worse.

## 4. What I have a solution for, with real values

1. **P16** `anneal_lr=1`, lr 1e-4 → 0 over the budget on a cosine (the binary has no linear schedule). Standard PPO; gives the missing stopping term.
2. **P15** `vf_clip_coef=0.2`. Standard; removes the critic's licence to own the trunk.
3. **P11** `anneal_ent_coef=1, ent_coef=0.01, min_ent_coef_ratio=0.1` (0.01 → 0.001). r11 ran this schedule.
4. **P8** `squash_chain_floor=0`. Exact chain; needs P7 so the policy is not parked at the rail.
5. **P1–P6** the contract decode: independent per-field heads to the gateway's own bounds (r54's decode, 0.227).
   This is the largest behavioural prior and removing it costs about 4% on the month today — the audit exists to
   make that trade visible, not to pretend it is free.
6. **P25** score the final checkpoint, or select on `atlanta_2`.
7. **P26** read the fp64 month only.
8. **r94b (proposed 2026-10-31, not approved yet):** r94's flags + a **linear** lr anneal from 1e-4 to 0 over the real
   50.14M budget. The binary's `anneal_lr=1` is cosine (`cosine_annealing`, `pufferl.cu`), so a linear schedule is a
   source arm (patch + `verify_all.sh --trainer`). Aimed exactly at r94-s75's late corner: over the last 10M the
   episode mean rose 0.072 → 0.189 and total entropy fell −181 → −235 with lr fixed at 1e-4, and the executed policy
   entered the 1.9 kPa / 0.96 / 348 K / run-on corner at 50.14M. If the SB_CREDIT sign audit says the corner is
   entered *with* the gradient (a real local optimum the sharpened policy falls into), the honest lever is this
   stopping term or budget-side changes, not any action shaping.

## 5. What I do not have a solution for

- **P20–P23 (hand-built observation and critic features).** The standard alternative is the raw gateway vector plus
  recurrence, and our own record says the features helped a lot (r14 0.856 without the nine features, r37 0.330 with
  them; the sibling block 0.174 vs 0.134). I have no standard replacement that is demonstrated, so I leave them
  listed, not removed.
- **P17 (Muon).** Adam alone was tested (r47) and lost on the month. Adam *with* P16 is untested.
- **P18 (rollout-wide advantage normalization).** The binary exposes 0 (off) and 2 (rollout-wide); per-minibatch is
  not implemented. That is a code gap, and I will not claim a flag value I cannot set.
- **P27 (Fairbanks-only).** The full batch was tested (r52) and went plant-off. No demonstrated alternative.
- **P12 (fresh-entropy estimator).** No measured comparison against the plain Gaussian entropy.

## 6. If removals are approved, the order

1. **P16 + P15** — the schedules. Cheapest, standard, and they give the agent a stopping term it does not have.
2. **P8 (+ P7)** — the rail: exact chain, and an amplitude that keeps the operating point out of saturation.
3. **P1–P5** — the decode. Largest behavioural prior; costs ~4% today.
4. **P11** — entropy anneal.
5. **P25** — selection.

One arm, one prior (AGENTS.md rule 4). Each removal updates this table with the measured cost.
