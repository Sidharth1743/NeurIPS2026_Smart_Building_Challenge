# 90-scenario held-out analysis

The measurement set: **5 buildings × 18 held-out weather windows = 90 deterministic 30-day months**
(8,640 steps each, 5-minute steps, all starting Sunday 06:00). Five policies are scored on it:
`pid_water` and four learned checkpoints (two r122 saves, two r116 saves).

Headline (lower is better; `score = −0.6·productivity + 0.2·energy + 0.2·carbon`):

| policy | score |
|---|---:|
| `pid_water` | 0.04609 |
| r122 @119,439,360 | 0.02723 |
| **r122 @89,579,520** | **0.02532** |
| r116 @50,135,040 | 0.03012 |
| r116 @45,121,536 | 0.03071 |

## Files, in reading order

| file | question it answers |
|---|---|
| `01_distribution.md` | What was selected? Building characteristics, weather severity, balance, score distribution, correlations. |
| `02_score_decomposition.md` | Where does each score come from? Terms, term contributions, per building/station/window, tail, variance split, save-to-save agreement. |
| `03_terms_and_fuel.md` | Comfort, energy and carbon in physical units; the per-building fuel shift behind the flat aggregate carbon. |
| `04_time_structure.md` | Month → day → day-part → hour. Where the score accrues and where the gap to the baseline comes from. |
| `05_actions.md` | What the policy does with 75–209 actuators: utilisation, concentration, chattering, the 18:00 shutdown, effective dimensionality. |
| `06_zones.md` | Where the scored comfort regret lives: per zone, per AHU, free float vs scored, cross-checkpoint stability. |
| `07_r116_appendix.md` | The r116 recipe on the same 90 months: paired deltas, where it wins, the opposite fuel trade, and what the comparison can and cannot claim. |

## Checkpoint breakdowns

`r139_199065600/` applies parts 1-3 of this suite (score decomposition, terms and fuel, time structure) to
the r139 checkpoint 199,065,600 on the same 90 months: **0.016514**, against r122 0.025315 (-34.8%) and
`pid_water` 0.046092 (-64.2%). See its README.

## Reproduce

```bash
# from the repo root, with the starter-kit venv (numpy/pandas/yaml)
K=starter-kit/.venv/bin/python
$K results/eval90_analysis/make_analysis.py       # 01, 02
$K results/eval90_analysis/make_terms_time.py     # 03, 04
$K results/eval90_analysis/make_actions.py        # 05
$K results/eval90_analysis/make_zones.py          # 06
$K results/eval90_analysis/make_r116_appendix.py  # 07
```

Every table in the seven markdown files is generated by these scripts from the raw artefacts
(`complete_port/artifacts/sets/eval5_heldout18`, the baked blobs, the station weather CSVs, the
per-step recordings under `complete_port/results/study_r122_eval90`, and the `pid_water` CSVs).
Nothing is typed by hand.

`data/` holds the machine-readable tables: `buildings.csv`, `windows.csv`, `scenarios.csv`
(90 rows × five policies + characteristics), `scores_by_{building,station,window}.csv`,
`tail_stats.csv`, `tail_shares.csv`, `variance.csv`, `correlations.csv`, `fuel.csv`,
`terms_by_building.csv`, `daypart.csv`, `hourly.csv`, `daily.csv`, `ramps.csv`, `first24h.csv`,
`action_roles.csv`, `action_diurnal.csv`, `action_pca.csv`, `action_utilization.csv`,
`action_distance.csv`, `zone_stats.csv`, `zone_ahu.csv`, `zone_cross.csv`, `zone_days.csv`,
`appendix_pairs.csv`.

## Validity

- Each scenario is **one deterministic episode**; there is no seed variance. Every distribution is
  across scenarios/time, not across runs. Two saves of the same recipe agree to 0.002 in score and
  rank scenarios at Pearson 0.96, which is the reproducibility evidence used instead.
- The canonical observation/reward decoding is validated against the recorded parts:
  mean comfort deviation 0.414305 vs 0.414324, worst zone 20.916 K both; the baseline's kWh
  reconstruction reproduces its `carbon_emitted` to <10⁻⁶ kg.
- `pid_water` has **no action or zone recording** (its CSV stores per-step reward terms only), so
  files 05 and 06 compare learned checkpoints among themselves; the baseline joins at the
  score/term/time level.
- Zone labels are canonical slots (AHU k, position j); the recording does not carry VAV names.
- The set is **not** station-balanced: Minneapolis is 8 of 18 windows, 44% of scenarios and about
  half the score. The station-equal mean (0.02364 vs `pid_water` 0.04317) is reported beside the
  window-weighted headline.
