## Reading

- r139 wins on the metric the competition scores, on the same 90 held-out months, by a margin larger than any r122 checkpoint had over the baseline.
- The win is not concentrated: it holds on all five buildings and both fuels fall, so it is not the electricity→gas trade that made r122's carbon flat (file 03).
- The large-plant tail is reduced but not removed: `train_3` is still 0.044 and its `minneapolis_1` month 0.085. That is the same tail the r122 analysis identified.
- Single checkpoint, one deterministic episode per scenario, this 90-month set: no seed variance and no leaderboard claim yet. The Kaggle row for this checkpoint is the external check.
